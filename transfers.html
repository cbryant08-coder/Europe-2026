Our booking strategy

🥇 Select First Class wherever the itinerary offers it.
🔄 Prefer 8–15 minute connections; 10–12 minutes is the comfort sweet spot.
⚠️ Avoid connections under 5 minutes unless clearly cross-platform and unavoidable.
🚶 Pay a little more or arrive a little later rather than rush through an unfamiliar station.
📱 Install SBB Mobile and add both travelers before Switzerland.
