🚻 Comfort strategy
The Circle, Hyatt, Airport Shopping and terminal areas all have dependable facilities. This is the easiest restroom day of the trip.

🍽️ Zurich Airport / The Circle Dining

🌱 Babel

  • Lebanese restaurant in the Hyatt complex with marked vegan mezze, falafel and salads.
  • Warm, casual-enough and the strongest sit-down fit without traveling into the city.
  • Ideal final-night meal when ease matters more than adding another outing.
Reservation: 🟡 Reservation helpful at dinner
Open Maps

🌱 Rice Up — The Circle

  • Build-your-own rice/noodle bowls with tofu and clearly marked vegan sauces.
  • Fast, casual and easy after the train from Montreux.
  • Best lunch or early-meal option; current listing closes around 5 PM.
Reservation: 🟢 Walk-in usually fine
Open Maps

🌱 Negishi Sushi Bar — The Circle

  • Clearly labeled vegan sushi, inari, edamame, miso soup and vegetable rolls.
  • Casual and dependable with later hours than Rice Up.
  • Good backup when you want an easy final meal.
Reservation: 🟢 Walk-in usually fine
Open Maps

🌱 Zoom Restaurant & Bar

  • Vegan wrap, planted-chicken dishes, soup and sides in the Hyatt Place area.
  • Useful backup when staying inside The Circle.
  • Choose it for convenience, not as a destination.
Reservation: 🟢 Walk-in usually fine
