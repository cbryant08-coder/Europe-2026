🍽️ Late lunch — Rue Cler / 7th arrondissement

Restaurant option
Use HappyCow/Maps live results near Rue Cler and choose the closest casual restaurant with a substantive vegan main; do not detour across Paris.
Backup

🍦 Sweet Stop

Choose a vegan pastry from Land & Monkeys or gelato where seating and atmosphere earn the stop.

Rue Cler’s food shops and the quieter Left Bank streets between Invalides and Champ de Mars.

💎 Hidden Gem

Square Rapp or the smaller side streets behind the tower—an intimate contrast to the broad lawns and crowds.

Buy only what you will use tonight; this is a good taxi-home day rather than forcing a grocery detour.
