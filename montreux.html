📍 Montreux Essentials — Suisse Majestic

🛒 Coop / Migros near Montreux station
Use whichever is closest and open for room snacks, bread, hummus, olives, chocolate, beer and wine. The hotel sits across from the station, so this is an easy homeward stop.
Open Maps
🥐 Station-area bakery
Choose the best vegan-labeled pastry available that day; Swiss bakeries vary, so confirm ingredients rather than assuming.
Open Maps
🚻 Comfort strategy
Dependable facilities: hotel, Montreux station, Château de Chillon, CGN boats and restaurants. The lakeside promenade has fewer guaranteed in-between options than Paris or Strasbourg.

🍽️ Montreux Dining Shortlist

🌱 Arctic Juice & Café

  • Health-focused café with vegan hummus wraps, roasted-vegetable bowls, quinoa salads and avocado toast.
  • Casual and easy for a light-to-medium breakfast or an uncomplicated late lunch.
  • Best daytime fit; closed Sunday according to current listing.
Reservation: 🟢 Walk-in usually fine
Open Maps

🌱 Bao Lab +

  • Casual Japanese-themed spot opposite the station.
  • Vegan gyoza, bao, poke, seaweed salad and miso soup provide enough choice for a satisfying meal.
  • Very convenient to the hotel and useful on arrival or before departure.
Reservation: 🟢 Walk-in usually fine
Open Maps

🌱 La Table de Montreux

  • Clearly labeled vegan bowl and avocado toast in a central, casual setting.
  • Not a destination restaurant, but a practical backup when hunger arrives.
  • Useful because of broad opening hours.
Reservation: 🟢 Walk-in usually fine
Open Maps

🌱 Bis

  • Several vegan mains plus starters, salads and dessert.
  • Better for an occasional dinner than your default rhythm.
  • Closed Sunday and Monday on the current listing—verify before relying on it.
Reservation: 🟡 Reservation helpful
Open Maps
Lakefront picnic
