⭐ Why this day works

Chillon supplies the major historical experience; the lakeside return supplies the atmosphere. A cruise would duplicate views rather than improve them.

🏰 Logistics
Hotel→ bus/train/walkChillon→ promenadeMontreux
Getting there: Use SBB Mobile the evening before for Montreux → Veytaux-Chillon. Choose the simplest bus or local-train option rather than walking the full distance before the visit.
Visit: Plan roughly 1½–2 hours. April–September hours are generally 9 AM–7 PM, with last entry at 6 PM.
Return: Walk part or all of the lakeside promenade back toward Montreux. If energy drops, board a local bus or train from Veytaux/Territet.

🍽️ Food strategy
Recommended: Return toward central Montreux for Arctic Juice or La Table rather than forcing a weak meal beside the castle.
Picnic: Bring supplies from Coop and use a public lakeside bench after the castle.
🚻 Restrooms
Hotel before leaving, Chillon visitor facilities, Territet/Montreux station and restaurants.

🚤 Lake Geneva cruise removed

The promenade, Chillon and lake viewpoints already provide the best parts of the experience. Lake Brienz remains the trip’s dedicated boat day.
