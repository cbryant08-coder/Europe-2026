⭐ Why it earns a day

The glacier, high-Alpine environment and railway journey are genuinely unique—but the price and time are justified only when visibility is excellent.

🚆 Route and logistics
Wengen→ trainKleine Scheidegg→ trainEigergletscher→ trainJungfraujoch
Outbound: Walk to Wengen station and follow the connection shown in the Jungfrau or SBB app. The route usually changes at Kleine Scheidegg and Eigergletscher.
Return: Reverse the route to Wengen. Begin descending by mid-afternoon so the day ends calmly at the hotel.
Tickets: Buy the Jungfraujoch ticket through the official Jungfrau site/app after the weather window becomes convincing. Seat reservations may be offered or required on the final mountain segment; select them if available.

🎯 Today’s Highlight

Walk toward the Eiger, Mönch and Jungfrau for most of the route rather than hiking to a single viewpoint.

🚠 Exactly how the day works
Hotel→ walkWengen cable car→ liftMännlichen→ walkKleine Scheidegg→ trainWengen
1. Get up: Walk from Hotel Bellevue to the Wengen–Männlichen cable car. Buy a one-way ride to Männlichen at the station or in the official app; advance reservation is normally unnecessary.
2. Optional Royal Walk: Add the short uphill summit walk only if visibility and energy are good.
3. Main walk: Follow the well-marked Panorama Trail toward Kleine Scheidegg. Allow 1½–2½ hours because you will stop often.
4. Lunch: Eat at Kleine Scheidegg or use your picnic.
5. Get home: Take the Wengernalp Railway from Kleine Scheidegg directly back to Wengen. No reservation is normally needed.

⭐ Why this is worth it

Mürren gives you a different angle on the same mountains and a quieter village atmosphere than Wengen.

🚆 Getting there and back
Wengen→ trainLauterbrunnen→ cable carGrütschalp→ mountain trainMürren
Outbound: Train Wengen → Lauterbrunnen. Follow signs for the Lauterbrunnen–Grütschalp cable car, then transfer directly to the mountain railway for Mürren.
Village: Mürren is car-free and compact. Walk the main lane, viewpoints and side streets; no local transport is needed.
Return: Mürren train → Grütschalp cable car → Lauterbrunnen train → Wengen. Start back by roughly 4:30–5:00 PM if you want hotel downtime before evening.
Tickets: Buy a Wengen–Mürren return ticket in SBB Mobile, the Jungfrau app, or at Wengen station. No seat reservations.

🎯 Today’s Highlight

See Staubbach Falls beside the village, then decide whether to continue along the valley floor.

🚆 Logistics
Wengen→ trainLauterbrunnen→ walk/busValley→ trainWengen
Outbound: Direct train from Wengen to Lauterbrunnen; buy a return ticket at the station or in SBB Mobile. No reservation.
Core route: Station → Staubbach Falls → village → riverside/valley path.
Trümmelbach option: Walk roughly 35–45 minutes along the valley or take the local bus from Lauterbrunnen. Buy entrance tickets on arrival; no advance booking needed.
Return: Bus or walk back to Lauterbrunnen station, then direct train uphill to Wengen. Aim for a train by late afternoon.

⭐ Recommended plan

Cruise one way from Interlaken Ost to Brienz, explore briefly, then return to Wengen by train. This avoids retracing the lake by boat.

🚆 Wengen to the boat and back
Wengen→ trainLauterbrunnen→ trainInterlaken Ost→ boatBrienz→ trainsWengen
Outbound: Train Wengen → Lauterbrunnen → Interlaken Ost. The Lake Brienz landing stage is beside Interlaken Ost station; follow the boat signs.
Cruise: Board a scheduled BLS boat from Interlaken Ost to Brienz. The boat calls at places such as Iseltwald and Giessbach depending on the sailing.
Preferred ending: Stay on to Brienz, walk the waterfront/old village, then take the train Brienz → Interlaken Ost → Lauterbrunnen → Wengen.
Return timing: Start the train return from Brienz by roughly 3:30–4:30 PM to reach Wengen around early evening.
BLS Lake Brienz info BLS tickets SBB timetable
🎟️ Tickets and reservations
Trains: Buy the complete Wengen → Interlaken Ost and Brienz → Wengen journeys in SBB Mobile before boarding. First class where offered.
Boat: Standard scheduled cruises normally do not require a seat reservation. Buy first class if you want the quieter upper deck/saloon. Purchase through BLS, SBB or at the pier once the weather is clear.
When: Decide 1–3 days ahead based on weather. You do not need to lock this in months ahead.
🍽️ Food and picnic
Preferred: Pack a picnic from Coop Wengen the evening before or buy supplies at Interlaken Ost before boarding.
Where to eat: On the boat if permitted/comfortable, at Brienz waterfront, or at a marked public bench/park.
Restaurant backup: Choose a casual café near Brienz station only after confirming a vegan dish.
